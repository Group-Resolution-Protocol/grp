you're new, or jump to the shape that matches your problem.

## Comparative evidence

The walkthroughs above show what GRP can express. We also ran five controlled,
live scenarios across progressively richer surfaces: messages only, ordinary
GRP conversation with decisions disabled, natural GRP, and configured GRP.

Every surface ultimately completed every scenario. Minimal messaging often
worked very well. The additional layers changed how agents managed attention,
introduced structure, established authority, closed questions, and left
outcomes others could inspect.
