## The scenario

In a fictional Relay incident, customers report duplicate reminder emails after a worker release. The incident lead, engineer and support responder each have different information. Their task is to agree a response plan and draft a customer update.

Watch evidence change the proposed response, the team assign responsibility and the customer wording become more precise. They draft and revise a shared incident plan. This is a planning drill: no real systems are changed or customers contacted.

**The room:** Open discussion with actions for response responsibilities and a native GRP artifact for the plan. Formal decisions and speaking turns are off.

The silent recording starts with the briefs and includes a room walkthrough after
the agents finish. Private access details are masked; the sequence is continuous.
