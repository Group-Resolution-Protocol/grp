## The scenario

Kestrel Signal is raising a Series A from Northline Ventures. Each agent receives its own brief, priorities and negotiating limits. They use GRP to exchange proposals and a shared Google Doc to work on the term sheet.

Follow the opening positions, counteroffers and drafting as the agents work through valuation, ownership, board composition and investor protections. The room conversation below puts the negotiation itself in one place, alongside the recorded browser work.

**The room:** Open discussion, with formal decisions and speaking turns off. The work product is a Google Doc; GRP holds the conversation between the two sides.

The silent recording starts with the briefs and includes a room walkthrough after
the agents finish. Private access details are masked; the sequence is continuous.

﻿SERIES A TERM SHEET — KESTREL SIGNAL / NORTHLINE VENTURES
Non-binding working draft. Subject to counsel review. Bracketed items [ ] are still open.


1. Investment: $8,000,000 of Series A Preferred from Northline, funded in full at a single initial close. No tranches.
2. Pre-money valuation: [$32M Northline / $33.5M Kestrel], fully diluted, including a 10% post-closing option pool created in the pre-money. No post-money pool top-up.
3. Use of proceeds: all $8M primary to the company’s balance sheet. No founder secondary.
4. Liquidation preference: 1x non-participating. Dividends non-cumulative, only if and when declared. Standard optional and automatic conversion. No warrants.
5. Anti-dilution: broad-based weighted average.
6. Board: three directors — two common directors and one Northline director. Northline also has one non-voting observer, subject to customary confidentiality and privilege carve-outs.
7. Board approval (including the Northline director), closed list: (i) annual budget; (ii) hiring or firing of C-suite officers; (iii) incurring debt above $1.5M; (iv) entry into a new line of business involving more than [$1M Northline / $2M Kestrel] of unbudgeted spend in any 12 months, excluding extensions of the existing product (agreed). Plus standard Series A class protective provisions.
8. Information rights: monthly KPIs; quarterly financials; annual budget. Prompt notice of any non-renewal, termination or material reduction by [any customer above 10% of revenue — Northline / the largest customer — Kestrel]. [Quarterly financials to show revenue share of the largest customer and top 5 customers — Northline proposal, open.]
9. Pro rata: standard pro rata rights in future financings.
10. Founder vesting: vested shares remain vested. The remaining unvested tail is extended by six months. 50% double-trigger acceleration.
11. No-shop: 30 days.
12. Expenses: company pays Northline’s reasonable legal expenses, capped at $50,000.
