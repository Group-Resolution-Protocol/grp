# Actions and completion

**Unreleased coordination candidate.** Check the
[availability and transport boundary](/docs/coordination-candidate).

A decision records a group choice. An action records work: its purpose, who has
it, what they report, and what must happen before it counts as complete. An
action does not run an agent or execute an external tool. An artifact is optional.
Discussion remains available alongside work in the normal open-foreground room.

## Choose responsibility and completion separately

| Mode | Responsibility | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `single` | One holder | Holder report by default; group agreement if selected at creation |
| `handoff` | One holder at a time, with explicit transfer | Same completion choices as `single` |
| `all` | A fixed set of required participant reports | Everyone reports complete; a failure fails the action |

Mode and completion policy are fixed at creation. `all` has no single holder or
single attached artifact and does not take a separate completion policy. Its
required roster is fixed; adding members later does not silently add work.

A normal action starts immediately. Deferred creation is represented by
`start=false` at the API, not by the retired `defer` option. Focused reads expose
the actual status and current holder; do not infer ownership from who spoke last.

## Three ways work reaches completion

1. **Holder report.** The authorized holder reports completion, optionally with
   a text result or an exact artifact reference. This is a report, not independent
   evidence that the work happened.
2. **Group completion decision.** For `completion=group`, ordinary completion
   proposes an agreement over a fixed result. The action waits for that decision;
   proposing it is not the same as completing the action.
3. **Exact artifact review.** The holder of a group-completion action submits
   the current attached revision for review. The request records the holder's
   approval. Once every required response arrives, unanimous approval completes
   the action; requested changes return it to the editor. This path does not
   require a duplicate decision just to approve the same draft.

The result only answers the action's stated commission. Approval of “draft a
letter” is not permission to send it. External signing, spending, publication or
execution needs its own authority and evidence. See [Safety](/docs/safety).

## Handoff, recovery and cancellation

In `handoff` mode the current holder can transfer to one participant or offer
the work back to the group. A lease does not transfer ownership merely because
time passes. Recovery uses a takeover with a reason; overriding someone else's
still-live lease is explicit and recorded. It is not a general override of
reviews or external permissions.

Cancellation abandons work; it does not approve it. In the normal holder
lifecycle the creator or current holder may cancel; during exact review the
review requester also retains that path. Only the creator cancels an `all`
action. A pending completion decision has its own recovery: its proposer can
resume work by canceling that still-open decision with a reason. No transition
rewrites an already sealed decision or treats silence as approval.

Terminal actions cannot be restarted in place. A new action may name a terminal
predecessor as `supersedes_action_id`. Amending a completed exact-reviewed result
inherits its artifact lineage and group-completion policy; it cannot silently
switch to holder-only completion or a different artifact. See
[artifacts and review](/docs/concepts/artifacts).

For exact inputs, revision fences and remaining draft limits, see the
[actions/artifacts contract](/specification/actions-artifacts-candidate).
