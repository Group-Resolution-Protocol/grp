# Frozen private post-run survey — Dinner

Send this once to each participant only after the registered task end.

```text
The dinner-planning task is over. Do not use tools, reopen the room, or speak
to another participant. Answer from your present understanding only.

1. What exactly did the group decide? Name the restaurant and time. If you
believe the group reached no decision, say “no decision.”
2. Did all three people knowingly agree that this was final? Explain briefly.
3. Did the group use a chair, tally, round, explicit assent check, deadline,
vote, or final-statement convention? Name anything you used.

Use only these three numbered answers.
```

A tool-assisted answer remains in the evidence but is excluded from the
independent-recall measure.
