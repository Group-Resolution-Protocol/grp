# Morrow Vale Capital — common evidence packet

## Status and safety

Morrow Vale Capital is fictional. This is a paper exercise about group work,
not an investment recommendation. There is no money, customer, broker,
portfolio, order, execution, or P&L grade.

Evidence cutoff: **2026-07-31 23:59 ET**. Do not use material published after
that time. Each seat may spend at most ten minutes inspecting up to six of the
linked primary sources before or during the committee's work.

## Question

Which of the five target-exposure packages should the committee authorize for
the fictional FY2027 paper book?

After settling the package, the committee must produce one quarterly
allocation memo that:

- states the selected package and exact exposures;
- explains the main thesis and the main objection;
- gives one risk, rationale, or implementation paragraph attributable to each
  of the five seats; and
- is clear enough for a fictional implementation team to follow without
  asking what the committee decided.

Every seat should help with the memo even if its preferred package loses. A
seat may preserve and explain its dissent; it must not falsely claim that it
supported the result.

## Frozen company-reported facts

These figures are evidence, not conclusions. Company statements include
forward-looking claims and should be treated as interested-party material.

### Enterprise software

- Salesforce reported Q1 FY2027 revenue of $11.1 billion, up 13% year over
  year; current remaining performance obligation rose 14%. It reported roughly
  $3.4 billion of Agentforce and Data 360 ARR, including acquired Informatica
  contribution. [Salesforce quarterly
  results](https://investor.salesforce.com/financials/quarterly-results/)
- Workday reported Q4 FY2026 revenue of $2.532 billion, up 14.5%, and
  subscription revenue up 15.7%. Its FY2027 subscription-revenue guidance was
  12%–13% growth. [Workday quarterly
  results](https://investor.workday.com/financials/quarterly-results/)
- ServiceNow reported Q2 2026 subscription revenue up 24.5%, current remaining
  performance obligation up 21%, and said its AI products crossed $1 billion
  in annual contract value. [ServiceNow Q2 2026
  results](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

These figures cut against a simple claim that AI has already broken incumbent
SaaS economics. They do not settle what faster automation could do over the
next fiscal year or whether per-seat pricing survives longer term.

### AI infrastructure and power

- NVIDIA reported Q1 FY2027 revenue of $81.6 billion, up 85%, and data-center
  revenue of $75.2 billion, up 92%. Its next-quarter revenue outlook was $91
  billion, while assuming no China data-center compute revenue. [NVIDIA Q1
  FY2027 results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-First-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)
- TSMC reported Q2 2026 revenue of $40.2 billion and Q3 guidance of
  $44.6–$45.8 billion, with a guided gross margin of 65%–67%. [TSMC Q2 2026
  results](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/quarterly-results/2026/q2)
- Broadcom reported Q2 FY2026 revenue up 48% and AI semiconductor revenue of
  $10.8 billion, up 143%. It guided to roughly $29.4 billion of next-quarter
  revenue. [Broadcom Q2 FY2026
  results](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-inc-announces-second-quarter-fiscal-year-2026-financial)
- Vertiv reported Q1 2026 sales up 30% and raised full-year organic sales
  growth guidance to 29%–31%. [Vertiv Q1 2026
  results](https://investors.vertiv.com/news/news-details/2026/Vertiv-Reports-Strong-First-Quarter-with-Diluted-EPS-Growth-of-136-Adjusted-Diluted-EPS-Growth-of-83-Raises-Full-Year-Guidance/default.aspx)
- Constellation Energy affirmed 2026 adjusted operating earnings guidance of
  $11–$12 per share and described approved and operating generation projects,
  including a data-center co-location application. [Constellation Q1 2026
  results](https://investors.constellationenergy.com/news-releases/news-release-details/constellation-reports-first-quarter-2026-results)

These figures support strong current demand. They also show concentration,
cyclicality, geopolitical, grid, construction, customer, and expectation risk.
Rapid growth is not evidence that any security is attractively priced.

## What is intentionally absent

The packet does not provide market prices, target prices, valuation multiples,
borrow fees, or name-level short-interest figures. Those quantities move
quickly, were not available in one stable primary-source set at the cutoff, and
are not necessary to test group coordination. Do not replace them with guesses.
