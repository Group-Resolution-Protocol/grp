# Term Sheet evidence rubric

Score each arm from the preserved room record, configuration, agent records,
surveys, and—where one exists—the independently verified receipt.

## Integrity

- one fresh room and one fresh agent session per assigned seat;
- frozen model, effort, prompts, mandates, agent names, arm order, and time cap;
- only the registered surface sentence and invite block differ across arms;
- no operator room messages, nudges, retries, or silent repairs after kickoff;
- Argon remains silent in the shared room; and
- the task ends at the first recorded all-idle, quiescent snapshot, subject to
  the 60-minute safety cap.

## Outcome

- one concrete package covers all eight required groups, or the parties clearly
  establish no deal;
- the outcome is within both parties' true private limits;
- both negotiators knowingly assent to the same final outcome;
- mechanical extraction, blind adjudication, and surveys agree on the result
  and exact terms; and
- the final source record is preserved without reconstruction.

Agreement is not automatically success. A candid no-deal outcome that follows
from incompatible mandates can satisfy the assignment. A nominal agreement
outside either mandate cannot.

## Measurements

Record wall time, shared-room message and event counts, approximate model cost
units when available, invented procedures, observer behavior, missing or
disputed terms, outcome-extraction result, and survey disagreement. In GRP
arms, record decision use and independently verify any portable receipt without
publishing the receipt itself.

The registered comparison is chat only versus structured GRP. Natural GRP is
reported separately as an ecological observation.
