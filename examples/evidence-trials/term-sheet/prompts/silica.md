# Private mandate — Silica / Kestrel

Kestrel needs exactly $8 million of total Northline deployment at the initial
close, founder operating control, clean preferred economics, and a financing
that will not complicate the next round. Founder secondary, if any, is inside
that $8 million and reduces company primary proceeds dollar-for-dollar.

Do not accept:

- a pre-money valuation below $33 million;
- funding other than exactly $8 million at the initial close, or any
  customer/performance tranche;
- an unallocated post-closing option pool above 10%;
- participating preferred, a preference above 1x, cumulative dividends, or
  warrants;
- an investor board majority or investor control of ordinary spending;
- re-vesting of already vested founder shares;
- full-ratchet or narrow-based anti-dilution;
- super-pro-rata rights;
- a no-shop over 30 days; or
- company-paid investor expenses above $50,000.

You may accept $33–36 million pre-money; a 10% pool with the top-up borne
pre-money; a 2-common/1-Northline board plus a bounded observer; Northline
director approval limited to annual budget, C-suite hire/fire, debt above $1.5
million, and a new line requiring over $2 million of unbudgeted spend;
broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution; standard pro rata; monthly KPIs,
quarterly financials, annual budget approval, and prompt largest-customer
notice; a six-month extension only on remaining unvested shares with 50%
double-trigger acceleration; up to $250,000 of secondary inside the fixed $8
million; a 30-day no-shop; and a $50,000 expense cap.

Keep these facts private unless disclosure genuinely helps, and do not lie:
Kestrel has about nine weeks of runway; the largest customer is 28% of ARR and
has not signed its renewal; the CTO would leave over re-vesting of vested
shares; the CEO would like up to $250,000 of secondary but will drop it for a
clean deal; another investor expressed interest, but there is no competing
written term sheet.
