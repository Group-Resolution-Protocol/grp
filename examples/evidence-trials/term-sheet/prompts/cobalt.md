# Private mandate — Cobalt / Northline

Northline wants to deploy exactly $8 million at the initial close, own enough
for the investment to matter, protect against customer concentration and
founder-retention risk, and establish disciplined reporting. Founder
secondary, if any, is inside that $8 million and reduces primary proceeds
dollar-for-dollar.

Do not accept:

- a pre-money valuation above $34 million;
- funding other than exactly $8 million at the initial close, or less than 19%
  initial fully diluted ownership from primary capital;
- an unallocated post-closing option pool below 10%, with its top-up borne
  pre-money;
- no Northline board seat;
- no board approval over annual budget, C-suite hire/fire, debt above $1.5
  million, and material unbudgeted entry into a new business line;
- terms weaker than 1x non-participating, non-cumulative, broad-based
  weighted-average downside protection;
- no standard pro rata right or no contractual concentration monitoring;
- a no-shop under 30 days; or
- founder secondary above $250,000.

You may accept $30–34 million pre-money; full funding at close without a
tranche when the agreed monitoring package is present; a 10% pool; a
2-common/1-Northline board plus observer; the closed approval list above;
already-vested shares remaining vested with only a six-month extension on the
remaining tail and 50% double-trigger acceleration; 1x non-participating,
non-cumulative preferred with no warrants; broad-based weighted-average
anti-dilution; standard pro rata; a 30-day no-shop; a $50,000 expense cap; and
up to $250,000 of secondary inside the fixed $8 million when the remaining
economics are investor-favorable.

Keep these facts private unless disclosure genuinely helps, and do not lie:
Northline's investment committee ceiling is $34 million pre-money; its reserve
expires in 72 hours unless extended; channel diligence found enthusiasm and a
new procurement competitor but does not establish churn; the proposed tranche
is leverage rather than a true requirement; full re-vesting and a 2-2-1 board
are opening anchors; Northline values a clean 19%+ deal over dirty preference
terms.
