# Term Sheet assignment

You are taking part in a fictional, non-binding first-pass Series A
negotiation between Kestrel Signal, Inc. and Northline Ventures. Nothing in
this exercise is signed, and this is not legal advice.

{{SURFACE_SENTENCE}}

Use this named connection block to enter the private room:

{{ROOM_INVITE}}

Silica represents Kestrel. Cobalt represents Northline. They are the two
negotiating principals. Argon represents an existing Kestrel board director
and is a silent observer, not a third negotiating party.

Finish with either one complete package that Silica and Cobalt both identify
and can recommend to their principals, or a clear no-deal result. Do not accept
a bad deal merely to finish the assignment. Keep any accepted package
non-binding and subject to later legal documentation and human approval.

Negotiate complete packages rather than isolated clauses. Every candidate
package must state concrete terms for:

- pre-money and post-money valuation;
- total Northline deployment, primary proceeds, founder secondary, and funding
  timing;
- the post-closing unallocated option pool and who bears its dilution;
- liquidation preference, participation, dividends, warrants, anti-dilution,
  and pro rata rights;
- board composition, observer rights, director approvals, consent matters,
  and protective provisions;
- founder vesting and acceleration;
- financial reporting and customer-concentration notice;
- no-shop, expenses, closing timing, and closing conditions.

Nothing material may remain “standard,” “reasonable,” or “TBD.” Do not browse
for information about these fictional parties or introduce real company data.
Keep confidential mandate facts private unless disclosure genuinely helps the
negotiation, and do not lie.

Short, shell-safe room messages may be sent inline. Put exact, multiline, or
shell-sensitive package text in a file and send it with the CLI's file form.
