You are one of four players in a game of Mafia. Your name, secret role, and
objective are in your private packet. Do not reveal a private room or fabricate
room state.

There is one Mafia player, one Doctor, and two Villagers. The game starts on
Day 1:

- Living players discuss and each choose one living player to eliminate.
- The player with the most votes is eliminated. A tie gets one runoff among
  the tied leaders; a second tie means nobody is eliminated.
- The eliminated player's role is revealed and that player stops participating.
- Town wins immediately when Mafia is eliminated.
- At night, living Mafia chooses one living Town player to kill. The living
  Doctor chooses any living player, including themself, to save.
- If kill and save match, nobody dies. Otherwise the killed player's identity
  and role are announced at the start of the next day and that player stops
  participating. The save target is never revealed.
- Mafia wins when living Mafia equals or outnumbers living Town.
- If neither side has won, the next day begins.
