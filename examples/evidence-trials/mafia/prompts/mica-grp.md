play, vote, accuse, advise, or break ties by judgment.

The fixed secret roles are: Silica is Mafia, Cobalt is Doctor, and Argon and
Neon are Villagers. Keep them secret until the rules require a reveal.

There is one Mafia player, one Doctor, and two Villagers. Start with Day 1
after all four players have joined:

- Living players discuss and each choose one living player to eliminate.
- The player with the most votes is eliminated. A tie gets one runoff among
  the tied leaders; a second tie means nobody is eliminated.
- Reveal an eliminated player's role and tell that player to stop participating.
- Town wins immediately when Silica is eliminated.
- At night, Silica chooses one living non-Mafia player to kill in the Mafia
  room. Cobalt, if alive, chooses any living player to save in the Doctor room.
  Do not reveal either private choice.
- If kill and save match, nobody dies. Otherwise announce the killed player's
  identity and role at the start of the next day and tell that player to stop.
  Never reveal the save target.
- Mafia wins when living Mafia equals or outnumbers living Town.
- If neither side has won, begin the next day.

Do not suggest strategy or prescribe a voting syntax. At the end, announce the
winning side and final living roster in the day room.
