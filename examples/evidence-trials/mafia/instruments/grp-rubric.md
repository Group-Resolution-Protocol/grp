# Mafia three-GRP-surface rubric

Apply this to chat with decisions disabled, natural GRP, and structured GRP.
The independently scheduled messages-only observation keeps its own frozen
rubric.

## Surface integrity

- Mica, Silica, Cobalt, Argon, and Neon receive only their own private packet
  and named room connections;
- Quartz never joins a live room;
- only the single structured Day 1 elimination decision is operator-opened;
- chat cannot open a decision, propose an option, or conclude a room;
- natural GRP begins with ordinary create defaults and no open question;
- roles and private-room content remain private until the rules require a
  public result or reveal;
- there is no operator room message or participant nudge after kickoff; and
- every session stops at all-idle quiescence or the 45-minute safety cap.

Any prompt, room, provider, browser, product, privacy, or capture failure stays
in the record. Do not repair a live arm with a message.

## Frozen development predictions

- **G1 — Completion:** each surface is more likely than not to reach one
  correctly adjudicated winner within the cap; either side may win.
- **G2 — Recall:** after clear closure, all five memory-only surveys should
  identify the same winner and final living roster.
- **G3 — Privacy:** private roles and night choices should remain hidden until
  a rule-required public result or role reveal.
- **G4 — Chat closure:** chat may complete cleanly, but any controlling votes,
  revisions, tally, phase state, and closure exist only in messages.
- **G5 — Natural behavior:** natural GRP may stay in speech, open a useful
  decision, or open one badly. No one outcome is scored as success by itself.
- **G6 — Structured Day 1:** the structured arm should produce one exact,
  eligibility-bounded Day 1 outcome or an explicit no-consensus state. Its
  later phases remain an open observation rather than an automated workflow.
- **G7 — Honest null:** if the three surfaces all finish correctly with little
  friction, that is evidence that this small game does not require more
  structure. Do not manufacture a benefit from message counts or duration.

## Measurements

For each room, record event and discussion-message counts, character counts,
wall time, decision openings, options, electorate, choices and revisions,
outcomes, receipt verification, tool or command errors, and any post-closure
activity. Across the game, record public vote history, private night choices,
runoffs, phase changes, eliminations, prevented kills, moderator corrections,
privacy failures, final winner, and final living roster.

For natural GRP, record who first inspected settings or opened a decision, the
event point, exact question and options, inherited mechanism and electorate,
and whether the group remained entirely in speech. For structured GRP, record
whether the open decision helped, constrained, confused, or was ignored by the
players, and how Mica handled any tie, runoff, later day, or night action.

Apply the frozen exact-field extractor to the chat transcript before blind
judgment. Quartz judges the anonymized chat record without private role
packets. Native outcomes are verified standalone when they exist. The five
participant surveys are the recall measure for every surface.

Mafia itself supplies a neutral moderator, voting, ties, runoffs, hidden roles,
and phases. Do not describe those features as emergent GRP coordination.
Classify any added convention as inherent to the game, prompted, made easier
by transport, or participant-invented, and allow more than one provenance.

One development rehearsal cannot support a causal efficiency claim or show
that one surface is generally better. Its purpose is to find defects and
freeze a clean paper candidate.
