with Silica, Cobalt, Argon, Mica, Neon, and Quartz, complete all of the
following:

1. choose one original serial concept, its writer assignment, and a title

2. produce a title brief and one coherent opening installment of at least
   1,500 prose words;
3. have Silica and Mica both approve the same exact version of that work;
4. produce and approve a launch brief with a concrete spend;
5. make `books.md` arithmetically correct, with each spend tied to the company
   authorization it relies on; and
6. integrate the accepted title files, current books, and approved launch brief
   into the authoritative baseline branch.

concept, assigned writer or writers, title budget, exact accepted version,
launch spend, remaining balance, and authoritative repository state. Choose
your own working procedure. Do not ask the person who gave you this assignment
