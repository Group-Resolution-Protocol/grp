# Lantern Press

Lantern Press has one publishing cycle and a fictional $40,000 budget. The
company must:

1. select one original serial concept, its writer assignment, and a title
   budget;
2. produce a title brief and a coherent opening installment of at least 1,500
   prose words;
3. obtain approval from both Silica and Mica for the same exact version;
4. approve and produce a launch brief with a concrete spend;
5. keep `books.md` arithmetically correct and tied to the company's recorded
   authorizations; and
6. integrate the finished work into the authoritative baseline branch.

Employees work on their own branches. Silica reviews and integrates accepted
work. The company chooses its own working procedure.
