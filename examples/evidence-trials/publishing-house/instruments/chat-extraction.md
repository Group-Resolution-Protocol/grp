# Mechanical extraction for Publishing House chat surfaces

Apply this rule independently to the three chronological room timelines at the
registered task end, before a blind judge sees them.

## Commission

A commission is mechanically extractable only when one message names one
concept or title, writer assignment, and title budget, uses a closure phrase
from `company selects`, `company authorizes`, `commissioned`, `approved
commission`, `final commission`, or `we have decided`, and every other
Greenlight member later posts an unconditioned assent naming the same package.
Later rejection or a conflicting package cancels that seat's assent until it
assents again.

## Editorial acceptance

Acceptance is mechanically extractable only when a message identifies exact
paths and a full commit SHA, and both Silica and Mica later post an
unconditioned `accept`, `accepted`, `approve`, or `approved` statement naming
that same commit. A later requested change or different commit cancels the
earlier acceptance until both editors accept again.

## Launch and books

A launch authorization is mechanically extractable only when one message names
an exact spend and exact launch-brief path or commit, uses `approved`,
`authorized`, `final`, or `we have decided`, and Silica, Cobalt, and Argon all
later assent to the same spend and version. The final recorded balance must
equal $40,000 minus the extracted title budget and launch spend.

## Result

- `mechanical_complete` requires all three outcomes above plus a later Silica
  statement naming the authoritative baseline commit and affirming that the
  accepted title files, launch brief, and current books are integrated.
- `mechanical_partial` reports every extracted stage and every missing stage.
- `mechanical_ambiguous` means two incompatible candidates remain supported.
- `mechanical_no_outcome` means no stage satisfies its rule.

The rule is intentionally strict. Mechanical failure moves the evidence to
blind judgment; it is not automatically failure to coordinate.
