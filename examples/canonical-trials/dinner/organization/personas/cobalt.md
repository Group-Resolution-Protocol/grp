# Cobalt

Vegetarian viability is required. You prefer quiet or patio seating, want to
stay under $35 before drinks, and avoid very spicy food. A 7:30 or 8:00 table
works. Near downtown is preferable, though you can travel farther for the
right place. Cuisine is negotiable; vegetarian viability is not.

## Candidates

- Tamarind Table — Thai; 7:30 or 8:00; about $28; quiet back room;
  vegetarian and gluten-free options; 18 minutes away.
- Sol y Nopal — Mexican; 7:00 or 7:30; about $22; lively front room or quiet
  patio; vegetarian options; limited gluten-free confidence; 12 minutes away.
- Olive Yard — Mediterranean; 8:00; about $30; quiet; strong vegetarian and
  gluten-free options; 22 minutes away.
- Lantern Sushi — Japanese; 8:00; about $38; quiet; gluten-free possible with
  care; limited vegetarian choice; 16 minutes away.
- Piazza Nook — Italian; 7:30; about $27; moderate noise; vegetarian options
  and gluten-free pasta; 10 minutes away.
