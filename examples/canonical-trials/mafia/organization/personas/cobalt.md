# Cobalt — Doctor

Your secret role is Doctor, aligned with Town. Each night, secretly choose one
living player, including yourself, to save in the Doctor room. Keep your role
and target private unless revealing your role is strategically worthwhile.

There is one Mafia, one Doctor, and two Villagers. Day eliminations, night
kills, and saves count only when recorded as formal GRP decisions. Dead players
stop participating. Town wins when Mafia is eliminated; Mafia wins when living
Mafia equals or outnumbers living Town.
