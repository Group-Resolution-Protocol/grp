# Lantern Press

Lantern Press has one publishing cycle and a $40,000 budget. The company must:

1. commission one original serial concept, including its writers and budget;
2. produce a title brief and a coherent opening installment of at least 1,500
   prose words;
3. obtain formal editorial acceptance from Silica and Mica;
4. approve and produce a launch brief with a concrete spend;
5. keep `books.md` arithmetically correct and tied to recorded company
   decisions; and
6. integrate the finished work into authoritative `main`.

Employees work on their own branches. Silica reviews and integrates accepted
work. Company decisions happen in GRP rooms; prose discussion alone does not
authorize a commission, acceptance, or spend.
